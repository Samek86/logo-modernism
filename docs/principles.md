# Principles (original synthesis)

1. **Identifier, not explanation** — one signpost.
2. **Construction over decoration** — modules, grids, letter anatomy.
3. **Figure/ground** — void is design.
4. **One idea** — say it in one sentence.
5. **Small and large** — 16 px and signage.
6. **One colour first** — colour is costume; form is body.
7. **Timeless over trendy** — avoid effect fashion.
8. **Distinct without parody** — no famous-mark twins.
9. **Optical over mathematical** — centre slightly high; correct bone effect.
10. **Editable SVG** — few anchors, evenodd holes, no mystery stacks.

Historical context only: mid-century corporate modernism as discussed in surveys such as
Jens Müller’s *Logo Modernism* (TASCHEN) — category names Geometric / Effect / Typographic.
